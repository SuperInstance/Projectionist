THE PROJECTIONIST

Demo levels for the text-frame renderer from Patchwork Experts. Every level plays side-by-side: the normal render on the left, live text frames on the right — with the full Studio sidebar, Director auto-composition, and recording.

LEVEL 1

Sail & Sink

A charming physics sailing game turned into a theatre of famous sinkings — Titanic, Britannic, Marmalade Run. Take the helm against impossible odds or bear witness while the director camera frames it for the text renderer.

Scrapcraft Story Cinema

The film starts itself — no buttons. A slow cinematic orbit through the Scrapcraft voxel yard, honouring a young author's storyline, mirrored live in ASCII beside the voxels with the full Studio sidebar of dials and telemetry. Grab the camera any time; ESC hands it back to the film.

Pocket Cinema
