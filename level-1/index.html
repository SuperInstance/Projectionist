Placeholder reward, not intelligence
The quality score combines edge density near a useful target, contrast use, and temporal stability:
0.40 edge band + 0.35 contrast + 0.25 stability
It is a legible starting signal for a future supervised policy, not a claim that the renderer knows what looks good.
Learner — best render settings per scene bucket, re-seeded with jitter every 20s. Variation, selection, memory; no watcher, no debt.

Built small enough to teach
Today the rules are visible: more motion means fewer columns and more frames. Tomorrow the same inputs can train something subtler.

        
What works now
Seeded islands, summed-sine waves, sampled buoyancy, deck friction, whirlpool pull, hull flooding, shared-frame text rendering, adaptive resolution, voyage clips and an exportable ring buffer.
What the bigger machines inherit
Train a compact policy from scene stats to render settings on GPU machines. Blend the documented heuristic with human preference labels, compare against the rule-based baseline, then keep only improvements people can actually see.
